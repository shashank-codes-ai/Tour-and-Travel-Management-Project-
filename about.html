The desk

A small desk for a large country.

Smart Tour & Travels plans routes across India, one state at a time.

Most trips fail in the joins: a fort at noon in May, a pass that is still shut, a ferry that left before the flight landed. This desk starts with those joins.

We keep the map to fifteen states — the ones people ask for first — and go deep on the places inside them. You get a written route, not a brochure of everything.

Prices on the site are starting points so you can compare shapes. The number that matters is the one in the plan, after dates and hotels are real.

Clear plans

Days, drives, and stays written in order.

Season honesty

If a road is closed, the route changes before you book.

Room to breathe

One main experience a day, not a checklist.

India only

Fifteen states, learned properly, rather than a world map.
