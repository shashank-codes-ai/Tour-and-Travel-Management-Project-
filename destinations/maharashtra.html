Gateway of India

Why go

A seafront city, rock-cut caves, and weekend ghats an hour from the coast. Maharashtra holds both a long weekend and a deeper art-history trip.

Ajanta is closed on Mondays. Ellora is closed on Tuesdays.

A sample route

Days 1–2

Mumbai — the Gateway, Marine Drive, and one neighbourhood walk.

Days 3–4

Aurangabad side — Ajanta and Ellora.

Day 5

Optional Lonavala if you are flying out of Mumbai.
