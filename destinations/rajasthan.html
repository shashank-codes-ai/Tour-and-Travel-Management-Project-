Rajasthan

From ₹18,999 · 7–10 days

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Why go

Palaces mirrored in still lakes, forts the colour of sunset, and desert nights under a huge sky. Rajasthan is India’s grandest stretch of royal cities.

Winter evenings get cool in the desert. Keep one unhurried day in Udaipur.

A sample route

Days 1–2

Jaipur — Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, old-city bazaars.

Days 3–4

Jodhpur — Mehrangarh and the blue lanes.

Day 5

Jaisalmer — the fort and a desert evening.

Days 6–7

Udaipur — lakes, palaces, and a slow last morning.
