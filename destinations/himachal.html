Himachal Pradesh

Manali

Why go

Pine ridges, colonial walks, and a cold desert if you go far enough. Himachal can be a gentle hill week or a high road into Spiti.

Rohtang and Spiti depend on snow and permits. We check both before locking dates.

A sample route

Days 1–2

Shimla — mall walks and a day in the cedar hills.

Days 3–5

Manali — old village, Solang, or a Rohtang day when open.

Days 6–8

Optional Spiti extension when the roads are open.
