Why go

Living temples, a cool hill station, and the point where two seas meet. Tamil Nadu is a strong pilgrimage route and a strong culture route.

Temple towns are busiest around festival dates. We plan darshan time in the morning.

A sample route

Days 1–2

Chennai or a direct start in Madurai.

Days 3–4

Madurai and a temple town onward.

Days 5–6

Ooty, or Rameswaram and Kanyakumari if the trip is coastal.
