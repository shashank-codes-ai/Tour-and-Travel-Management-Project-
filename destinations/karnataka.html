Hampi

Why go

Boulder-strewn ruins, a palace city, and coffee country. Karnataka suits travellers who want ruins one day and green hills the next.

Hampi is hot by midday. Start the ruins early and keep the afternoon for the river.

A sample route

Days 1–3

Hampi — ruins by foot and coracle.

Day 4

Drive toward Mysuru or a coffee stop in Coorg.

Days 5–6

Mysuru, or the coast at Gokarna if that is the mood.
