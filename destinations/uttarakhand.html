Why go

The Ganga at Rishikesh, lake towns, and meadows that open after the snow. Uttarakhand is the easy mountain state from Delhi.

Valley of Flowers is a trek and opens roughly July to September.

A sample route

Days 1–2

Rishikesh — the river, a short rafting stretch, and the evening aarti.

Days 3–4

Mussoorie or Nainital for a lake-and-ridge break.

Days 5–7

Auli or a Valley of Flowers window, by season.
