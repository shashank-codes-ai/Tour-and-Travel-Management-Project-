Questions

Before you write to us.

Do you only plan trips inside India?

Yes. This site covers India’s 15 most-visited states and the places inside them. Routes can combine two states when the travel time makes sense.

Are the prices on this site final?

They are indicative starting prices per person, usually on a twin-share basis. Season, hotels, and flights move the number. A written plan comes before you pay anything.

Can the contact form send my message?

Not yet. The form is on the page so you can see the fields. For now, use the WhatsApp button or the phone and email in the footer.

How many days should I keep for Ladakh or the Andamans?

Ladakh wants at least a week including rest days in Leh. The Andamans want about six to eight days because ferries set the pace.

Do you arrange darshan and permits?

Temple timing and permit-based places such as Nathu La, Rohtang, and parts of Spiti are checked before a route is confirmed.

Is the site usable on a phone?

Yes. The layout, menu, galleries, and form are built to work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

What is included in a package?

Stays, private or shared transport as written, breakfast, and the experiences listed for that package. Flights are quoted separately unless the plan says otherwise.

Can I mix two packages?

Yes. A honeymoon week can add a family-friendly stop, and a pilgrimage route can slow down into a luxury stay. Tell us the mix and we shape one plan.
